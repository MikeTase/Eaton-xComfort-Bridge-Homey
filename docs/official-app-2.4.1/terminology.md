# Official Eaton terminology (en / nl / de / no)

From `assets/i18n/*.json` of the official app 2.4.1. Homey's custom capabilities currently have `en`/`nl` titles only,
although the app ships `da`, `de`, `no` and `sv` locales. These are Eaton's own wordings. They are useful for filling `de`/`no` and for
keeping `nl` consistent with what users see in the Eaton app. The official app has no Danish or Swedish translations.

Note: Eaton calls the lowest preset **Protection** (nl *Bescherming*, de *Schutz*, no *Frostsikring*), where Homey uses *Frost / Vorst*.
